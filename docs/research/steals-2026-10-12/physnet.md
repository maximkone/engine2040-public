=== 5. NOT WORTH IT ===
- Box2D/Box3D soft-step solver or the Box3D engine as the debris solver: the 3D path is Rapier and the old solver.rs (4 substeps x 10 iterations, solver.rs:9,38) is the frozen golden path; a second physics backend is CUT (body-and-break.md:81,95). Read as reference only.
- A fixed-point rigid-body engine: search found no active Rust one (only a BEPU Fix64 port and box3d-rust, a float port); our tick is integer for limbs/balance and ordered f32 + - * / sqrt elsewhere.
- Runtime mesh slicing of skinned meshes (EzySlice-style or the paid "Slice SkeletalMesh" plugin): duplicates mesh and skeleton, float result differs per GPU/CPU, no replay story. The plan's plane clip is better.
- Stencil capping on every stump: 3 extra passes per stump for a result the two-sided fill gives free on closed segments; keep as a hole fallback only.
- A rewind-style lag compensation for the duel: open sword games (e.g. vivekch91/Sword_Fighting) skip it; with rollback it is redundant. Keep lagcomp for the siege only.
- Custom trig like Box2D b2ComputeCosSin / Jolt Sin: we already ship Fixed LUT + CORDIC atan2 (interp.rs:22-24).
