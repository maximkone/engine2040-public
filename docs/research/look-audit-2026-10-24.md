## Top 10 NEW defects, briefs for fixers

1. **Weapon trail is an opaque white sheet (row 1).** `audit-play/frame_000007.png`, `audit-spear/frame_000007.png`. In `trail_draw_gpu.rs` and `trail.wgsl` fade alpha to about 0.25 at the newest pair, 0 at the oldest, tint with the weapon's steel colour, and test with `--hold W,F` for 700 frames.
2. **Legionary first person: start in the wall, own helmet and shield fill the view (row 2).** `audit-legion/frame_000000.png`, `_000007.png`, `_000008.png`. Spawn the eye facing the rival, hide the wearer's galea and scutum in the viewmodel pass, give the gladius a hand and forearm, add a crosshair; extend `eye_in_body` so a near-plane cover over 30 percent fails.
3. **Camera loses the fight (row 5).** `audit-spear/frame_000008.png`, `audit-staged/frame_000004.png`, `audit-play/frame_000011.png`. Frame the pair's midpoint plus rope anchor with a minimum on-screen height (fighters at least 25 percent of frame height), keep airborne fighters in shot, and on the player's death follow the killer not the corpse.
4. **Skin holes in the hero's armour (row 7).** `audit-cam/frame_000011.png`. The back, flanks, elbow and hips show pink triangles where the shell leaves the skin mesh visible; close the shell or hide the skin under it, and give the helmet a back (the head is a smooth egg from behind).
5. **Blood and dust are hard squares (row 8).** `audit-spear/frame_000010.png`, `audit-legion/frame_000007.png`. The flipbook cell sampled in `particle.wgsl` is flat; bind the real round soft atlas for the arena window, check `particle_draw_gpu.rs` and `content/vfx`, and shrink blood to 6 to 12 px with a short stretch along velocity.
6. **Rope and spear drawn as planks and wire (row 9).** `audit-tour/frame_000012.png`, `_000010.png`, `audit-spear/frame_000005.png`. Draw the tether as a smooth sagging cable (catenary through 16 or more points, 2 to 3 cm thick, a rope material), end it at the hand, and keep the tow pole from running off both edges of the frame.
7. **Cape is a shard, a stub or a pink flap (row 10).** `audit-tour/frame_000000.png`, `_000007.png`, `audit-play/frame_000009.png`. After PX44c verify the cape hangs from both shoulders and reads deep red in every light; settle the cloth for 20 ticks before the first shot so tick 1 is not a spike.
8. **Hit flash turns the hero into salmon clay (row 11).** `audit-play/frame_000006.png`. Limit the tint to a 20 percent additive pulse on the struck limb (or a rim flash), 0.1 s long, instead of replacing the whole armour colour for 0.25 s.
9. **Grey trapezoid on the sand at the wall foot (row 12).** `audit-tour/frame_000011.png`, `audit-spear/frame_000010.png`. A world-fixed hard-edged 3 x 1 m grey quad with no caster; bisect by hiding `gate_tunnel` (placement id 5), the stands lip and the banners, then fix or delete it.
10. **Hero and rival do not share an art style (row 13).** `audit-tour/frame_000010.png`, `audit-play/frame_000009.png`. The hero is a flat white mannequin with no metal response; give the armour a metal roughness map plus a real face or visor from behind and in front so both bodies sit in the same lighting model.

Also NEW but below the cut: HUD bare bars (row 18), speed-demo dunes (row 21), cape/body and body/body interpenetration (row 17).

## Notes for whoever runs the next audit

- `--frames N --exit` has a 300 s wall-clock cap; on a shared GPU a 1200-frame capture run does not finish (rows 22), so use `--capture-every 100` and at most 1000 frames per run until capture-mode perf lands.
- A capture at the title (no `--play`) produces zero frames; use `--play` or a takeover flag.
- The window hot-reloads `crates/render/src/shaders/forward.wgsl`: another agent's half-saved edit produced `redefinition of map_texel` mid-run (it kept the last good shader, so frames were unaffected).
