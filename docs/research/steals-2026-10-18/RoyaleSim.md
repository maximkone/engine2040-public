## What it is

An integer-only, 20 Hz battle simulation of one game, exposed to Python through pyo3 (`royalesim.Battle`: reset/step/state_json/catalogue_json) so reinforcement-learning bots play it with no game installed. It is "whole numbers only" by construction (README badge) and its docs carry the measurements: a replay-parity report against recorded client battles, and a hash-continuity test that compares one build's battle hashes against its parent's.
