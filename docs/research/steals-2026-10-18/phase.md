## What it is

A production card-game rules engine: ~2,950 files under crates/engine (parser, game, types, database), an AI crate (crates/phase-ai) with search, eval, combat AI and a committed strength baseline, a server (crates/server-core, crates/phase-server) and a WASM + React client. The engine is what is worth reading: rules are data-driven, the AI can only propose actions the engine already minted (an `AiDecisionContract`), and AI strength and decision cost are gated in CI against a committed baseline file.
