## What it is

A Bevy-ECS engine whose pitch is huge GPU physics plus "determinism, replay and an agent-friendly CLI". Three choices stand out: a determinism *ladder* the whole simulation declares into, a determinism CI that compares debug/release/one-thread/GPU runs tick by tick, and a JSON-lines debug session that lets an agent drive a running game without restarting it.
