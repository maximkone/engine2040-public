- Unauthenticated sockets: bevy_remote and puffin_http speak TCP with no auth; the harness binds 127.0.0.1 only. Our door is stdio. Any HTTP surface binds 127.0.0.1 and ships off in release builds.
- Harness maturity: 2 stars, one author, last push 2026-10-09. Its ADRs are the useful part; copy the shapes. The owner's order sets no license gate.
