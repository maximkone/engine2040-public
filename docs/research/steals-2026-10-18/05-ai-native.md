## Traps

- Agentick's default ASCII mode is ANSI-colored; strip escape codes before the model sees the grid or it pays tokens for them.
- Oracle normalization needs a real optimum. A fight has no closed optimum: name the anchor (best scripted policy), print it, and never invent a maximum.
- The 3-10x reasoning claim is the paper's on its own models. Measure on our free models before making the reasoner preset a default. Reasoning text is provenance only and never enters the tick; the action parse takes one integer.
- Weak and free models: keep tool lists small (the 13-core-tool rule), and a reasoner preset costs tokens.
- Licenses are info only (owner 2026-10-10), but ftl-bench has no license and ogremcp platform is AGPL-3.0: take the idea, not the bytes. Summer desktop app is closed; only the MIT repo is in scope.
- Staleness: ClaudePlayer last push 2025-03; AriGraph 2024-09; Agentick 13 stars (soak before trust). Summer page gives 37 and 44 tool counts (conflict; unverified).
