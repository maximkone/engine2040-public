## Traps

- WebGPU: depth textures cannot use a filtering sampler. Any SSR or depth-read pass needs the manual texel-fetch branch (Bevy's `USE_DEPTH_SAMPLERS` false path, seen in raymarch.wesl). Decide before RT19 starts.
- WebGPU has no geometry shader: Veloren `light-shadows-geom.glsl` is out. Row 2 is fragment-only and web-safe.
- Readback: Fyrox's occlusion reads its mask back to the CPU. post.md #7 bars live-frame readback stalls. Take row 6's cell memory, keep the GPU HiZ.
- Row 4 cost: about 11 full-screen 9-tap passes at 1080p. Run at half resolution or skip on potato. Row 1 is the potato outline path.
- Row 1 needs a cooked outline normal per fighter vertex. Hard corners need a smoothed normal baked at cook (split-normal seams must not show in the outline).
- Row 3 is a second tonemap candidate. Do not land it beside PBR Neutral: pick one curve (post.md XX1) by the torch-flame hue test.
- Float: every row is render-side. None touches a sim crate or the golden hash.
- Not verified: frame cost of every row on the RTX 3050 or the Intel UHD. Nothing built.
