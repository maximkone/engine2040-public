## Traps

- The sim is integer. Ports go in mm, ms and ticks (controller2.rs uses mm/s and TICKS_PER_S). Tnua's segmented energy calc needs sqrt: use an integer isqrt in the tick, never f32. Fyrox's Delaunay uses spade (float): port the topology with i64 orient tests, no crate in the tick.
- Donor numbers are donor units. Tnua's extra gravities are added to world gravity; retune for our 22 m/s^2 (GRAVITY_MM_S2 22_000) before any number moves. Our jump tap/hold apex is unmeasured today.
- Veloren's parry line is probably a bug as written: block_strength is set to damage just before poise_multiplier = 2 - min(damage/block_strength, 1), which evaluates to 1.0. Copy the intent (a parry answers a full hit), not the line.
- Veloren poise uses f32 with u32 x256 storage; the thresholds 50/30/15/5 are absolute points, and our PoiseMeter default max of 100 (depth.rs:38) is on the same scale.
- Bevy easing and Fyrox spade code are float. Presentation only (XX2 FOV punch, XX4 telegraph, XX6 look ease); never hashed.
- Flank precision, the armor soft cap and the block poise numbers change damage: a design call for the owner, not view work.
- Rows 1 and 2 touch the fight feel first; rows 3 and 9 are cheap wins with no sim change.
