Rules (owner, 2026-09-29):

1. Repos: take the best fit for the part furthest from its bar. `pin` is the commit read (`T` = it has tests or CI); `reads` are the files and first matching line: open them at that pin and write ONE card (what it does, where it fits, source@sha `path:line`, proof command, net lines).
2. Patterns: a file that holds the pattern itself. Read the file at its repo's HEAD, take the idea, and write it as a card or a spike; same licence rules.
3. Models and write-ups: a model is a tool our products can call or bake in (licence rules below); a write-up is another team's lesson, read it before designing the same thing. A card or an Avoid line retires it.
4. Avoid: closed issues and reverts other projects paid for. Each relevant one becomes one Avoid line with a guard (a test, a check or a drift rule) in your own repo; naming it removes it from here.
5. `code-ok` (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC, Unlicense, Zlib): code may be adapted with attribution. `idea-only` (GPL, other copyleft): the idea, never the code.
6. A bad lead is dropped with one line why. A good card becomes a small spike with a baseline (card, spike, row, landed, bar). No random copy, no spam.
