## CARD 1 — TAKE (MIT, adapt with attribution) — WGSL cloud slab: layer-intersect + FBM density + dual-lobe HG + light-cone march + Beer-Lambert, quarter-res + temporal cap

- Source: `nff747/volumetric-clouds-wgsl@1096ad7` `src/shaders/cloudMarch.wgsl.ts` — HOW THEY HANDLE it: (a) SLAB intersect first (`tNear/tFar` from `[cloudBottom,cloudTop]`, early-out when `worldDir.y <= 0.001` or `tFar <= tNear` — empty sky costs one slab test); (b) DENSITY is analytic, not textured (`heightGrad = sin(h·π)` × 4-octave FBM at `pos·0.0006` + wind drift `time·0.02`, minus `(1−coverage)`, clamped × `densityMultiplier` — every knob is one uniform, no 3D texture cook); (c) LIGHT is a secondary march (fixed 60 m light steps toward sun, Beer-Lambert `exp(−τ·0.04)` with powder-sugar `(1−exp(−τ·0.08))`, dual-lobe HG `0.75·fwd(g=0.82) + 0.25·bwd(g=−0.22)`, warm sun + height-graded blue ambient); (d) EARLY-OUT on transmittance (`break` at `T < 0.01`, step clamped to `(tFar−tNear)/maxSteps`). What they cut: no 3D density cache (jeantimex bakes one — nff747 evaluates FBM inline, cheaper memory, more ALU), no temporal reprojection, no half/quarter-res pass, no erosion/detail octave — full-res fixed-step march every frame (the cost hole; the paper fills it). What their tests cover: `tests/clouds.test.ts` + `weather/jitter/volumetricRaysPass/crepuscularRays.test.ts` under vitest (uniform/param shapes + jitter + pass wiring — CPU-side contracts, not pixel goldens).
