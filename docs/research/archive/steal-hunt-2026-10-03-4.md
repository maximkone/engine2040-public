## Card 1 — impact-seeded runtime Voronoi shatter with volume receipt

- Source: http://www.4rknova.com/blog/2026/07/27/voronoi-fracture (2026-07-27, 70-min guide
  + interactive demo; fetched live 2026-10-03) | License: UNVERIFIED (blog prose, ideas-only;
  demo is three.js, nothing vendored — reimplement in Rust).
- What it does: shatter a convex mesh at runtime by clipping against perpendicular bisector
  planes of seed pairs (`bisectorPlane(si,sj)`, normal sj-si through midpoint). Load-bearing
  details: (a) seeds sized by distance falloff exponent 4 — small shards near impact, large
  far; (b) on convex solids the cut leaves provably one cap loop, so capping is a fan, not
  a search; concave needs a different representation (explicit non-goal); (c) verify by the
  divergence theorem — shard volumes must sum to the pre-fracture volume; (d) shards fly on
  ballistic arcs with NO inter-shard collision (declared cut, keeps it in budget).
- Home: `crates/destruction/src/csg.rs:392` (`knife` gains a bounded multi-plane entry) fed
  by `crates/destruction/src/debris.rs:203` `wake()` (runtime shards beside pre-broken ones).
  No home for concave — out of scope by donor's own limit.
- Fixes: BX runtime-fracture leg (bake is cook-time only; `knife` is one plane, uncapped,
  unseeded) — Core Bodies axis, deterministic collapse.
- Net lines: ~150 (seed sampler + bisector clip loop over `split_mesh` + cap fan + volume sum).
- Proof: fixture convex prop + impact point, 9/25 seeds, falloff-4 vs uniform ablation;
  assert volume drift <= 1e-3, tris <= `MAX_BAKE_TRIS`, digest identical x2; no proof = reject.
- Kill: volume drifts once, or tri count over cap, or digest differs across 2 runs, or any
  tick byte drifts (shards enter via existing `wake` path).
- Effort/risk: S (reuses `split_mesh`); risk is cap winding on near-degenerate cuts — refused
  via existing `DegenerateCut`, never silent.
- Plan prefix: BX (body-and-break.md runtime fracture) | Core: Bodies | EMPIRE: proof-and-pull.

## Card 2 — ordered canonical digest replacing the XOR state fold

- Source: Loci2D/loci2d@77cbbc2b60e013e69a125b3b3846a5089d9868c3 `src/replay/hash.rs`
  (read live 2026-10-03; header: canonical hashing for determinism verification + replay
  checkpoints, ADR-0007/ADR-0010) | License: UNVERIFIED (LICENSE file unread in budget;
  ideas-only, std-only reimplement, zero bytes vendored).
- What it does: length-prefixed, big-endian, sorted-key-order digest over raw integer bits
  of fixed-point state (tick, match state, globals, timers, entities in BTreeMap key order,
  `to_bits()` per coordinate), with tests proving insertion-order independence and
  tick-sensitivity. The transferable shape is ordering + length-prefixing + integer bits,
  not SHA-256 specifically (engine-core stays std-only: same discipline over our u64).
- Home: `crates/arena/src/lib.rs:1292-1299` (production multi-controller hash folds with
  `hash ^= controller.state_hash()`) and `:3051-3055` (test fold). XOR is commutative but
  lossy: two identical sub-hashes cancel (`a ^ a = 0`) — twin fighters in identical state
  contribute nothing, and a 3-fighter permutation collision class is wider than ordered concat.
- Fixes: BX28 replay-exact hash axis (one hash at every setting) — Core Bodies.
- Net lines: ~40 (ordered fold helper + twin-controller test).
- Proof: fixture with 2 identical controllers: XOR fold 0 (blind) vs ordered digest distinct
  from the 1-controller digest; insertion-permuted world identical digest x2; no proof = reject.
- Kill: ordered digest collides on the twin fixture, or costs over the per-tick hash budget,
  or any golden changes for a reason other than the intended strengthening.
- Effort/risk: XS; risk is golden churn — expected, one-line receipt updates with reason.
- Plan prefix: BX28 (body-and-break.md replay hash) | Core: Bodies | EMPIRE: proof-and-pull.
