## HOW THEY HANDLE it (mechanism notes)

- SkinGore: hit world-pos → root-bone-local (`InverseTransformPoint`, `SkinGoreRenderer.cs:AddDamage`); damage baked by an ortho camera into a 64px `goreMap` UV-space texture; dilate pass pads the spot, additive blend accumulates into the persistent map; temp textures pooled; `TransferToNewGoreRenderer` copies/blits the map so the ragdoll keeps the living fighter's wounds. Cost is per-hit bake, no per-frame RT.
- Looman: decals slide on skinned meshes (PUBG failure datum); fix = record hits in pre-skinned/bone-relative space (world → bone-inverse → reference-pose) and evaluate 3–5 fixed sphere-mask slots in-shader; spread/dry animated by scaling the mask over time. Render-target-per-character variant measured 1.6–4.5 ms spikes on GTX 850M — killed by cost, slots win.
- oxihuman: `TearConstraint{particle_a, particle_b, rest_length, max_stretch, broken}`; `step_tear_simulation` integrates semi-implicit Euler per substep, 1 PBD iteration, `stretch > max_stretch → broken=true` (never healed); `tear_at_point` breaks constraints by midpoint radius; `count_broken/intact` counters are the test seam.
- arXiv 2209.08531: unconstrained progressive cuts/tears on rigged soft-body meshes in ~10 ms via continuous fracture along the tool path (ideas-only; our transfer is tick-side only: a blade drag = bounded sequence of integer cut events, topology stays presentation).
