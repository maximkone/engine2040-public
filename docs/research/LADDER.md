# Research ladder — every idea, one line, climbing

> Read one rung only (e.g. `Select-String -Pattern "^- CARD" docs/research/LADDER.md`). Never read this file whole (~424 KB); history lives in git.

Plan: `docs/plan/frontier.md`. Owner 2026-09-27: research must make the engine
futuristic, AI-agnostic, fast, good-looking and spatially smart, and every idea
has to earn its place. Readers add `CARD` lines; the experimentalist or the
benchmark-engineer adds the spike numbers; the orchestrator moves a line to
`ROW`, `LANDED` and `BAR`. The retro counts lines per rung
(`node tools/ladder-counts.mjs`) and reports research tokens per landed row. Per-wave research files (`arxiv-wave*.md`,
`steal-wave*.md`) stop here: history stays, new work is a line.

Rungs: `CARD` -> `SPIKE` -> `ROW` -> `LANDED` -> `BAR`, or `REJECTED` (reason and
re-open signal). A spike outranks a new card: while a CARD marked ACCEPTED waits,
the wave carries one EXPERIMENT (or BENCH) packet for it.

Line format:
`- <RUNG> | P<pillar 1-5> | <idea in a few words> | <source: arXiv ID / repo@rev / talk> | <fixture, baseline -> candidate, numbers> | <row, SHA, bar or reason>`
