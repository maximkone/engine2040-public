## Surprises

1. **`board-slice` cannot see 48 open rows.** Its `HEAD` regex
   (`/^- \[( |x)\] ([A-Z][A-Z0-9]*\d[a-z]?\d?)\b/`) rejects any id with a hyphen.
   So every `E-*` inbox row, `BET-*` and `CQ-NET-*` open row is invisible to
   `--ready`, `--open`, `--focus` and `--find`. Command count 801 vs tool 754.
   The handoff already flags this class (`NS31b1`, `GEO1a`).
2. **96 parked rows are still counted as "open"**, and 29 open rows depend on a
   parked row, so the "ready" pipeline has permanent dead ends (viewer BV chain,
   AW chain, RT chain). Three of the top-30 blockers are parked rows.
3. **Every one of the 32 obsolete E-rows is still open.** The owner close-out of
   `2026-10-17-obsolete-rows.md` has not happened yet; 26 of the 111 open rows in
   `10-empire-inbox-drain` are already parked, and the 32 obsolete ones sit open
   beside them. That shard is the largest single backlog (111 open).
4. **The triage's "gone file" set shrank fast.** Since the 2026-10-18 triage,
   WIN8, RA16, PQ21, GP8, AD8, EG44, RC14, WEB9, QR2, QR7 and CQ14 are all ticked
   (restores landed or rows closed REPLACED). The triage's claim "PW41 open"
   stands for PX41 and E38, but GP8 closed without restoring `camera_feel.rs`.
5. **`14-agent-workspace` is 21 parked of 22 open** (only AW9 is not parked), yet
   its AW12/AW18 lead the tools frontier: the shard is parked but still blocks
   work.
6. **Orphan content still in the tree**: `fog_vol.wgsl` and `skin.wgsl` shaders
   and `content/fighters/window_bind.json` have no Rust reader.
7. Handoff said 808 open rows; the board is at 801 (7 closed since).
