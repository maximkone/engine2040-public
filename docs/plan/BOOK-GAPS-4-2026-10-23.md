# Book gaps 4, speed and mobile (2026-10-23)

Owner 2026-10-23: read the Downloads books again, compare them with the
engine, find the gaps, and add the optimization and mobile side ("best
Windows and mobile optimized af, AI-native").

This is the fourth ledger. It does not repeat `BOOK-GAPS-2026-10-12.md`
(W/S/D rows), `BOOK-GAPS-2-2026-10-12.md` (N1-N40) or
`BOOK-GAPS-3-2026-10-19.md` (D/G/R and NB1-NB5). Where a later landing
closed an older line, section 7 says so.

**Scope change.** `finish-line.md` lists mobile as a non-goal ("no mobile
or console targets"). This pass treats the owner's 2026-10-23 message as
the override: mobile becomes a target tier beside Windows and the browser.
It is a tier, not a port of every feature. Android first; iOS waits for a
Mac in the loop.

## 1. Method and coverage

- 40 books already have text extracts in
  `C:\empire\lab\skillworks\work\<slug>\full_text.txt` (gitignored; no book
  text is committed).
- 11 design and PCG books have no fresh extract; their 2026-10-12 reader
  notes in `work\nb-gaps\` were re-read and spot-checked against the PDFs.
- Every book's section map was read. About 200 targeted sections were read
  closely. No book was read cover to cover; a full pass over 40 books is
  not a useful diff against a near-finished engine.
- Every surviving claim was checked in the live tree with `rg`/`Read` and
  against `node tools/board-slice.mjs <ID>` today. Old line numbers were
  not trusted.
- Result in one line: the shelves are drained. The engine is ahead of
  Beuken, Eberly, Zerbst and most of Gems. What remains is integration
  seams, a few protocol faces, and the new mobile/optimization lane.
