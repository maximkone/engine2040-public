# KAM2 - Knights-and-Merchants economy, Red-Alert-2 look, Rust, in this engine

Owner order 2026-10-20: recreate KaM Remake in Rust in RA2 art style, autonomously,
until it is a game; polish engine2040 gaps in parallel.

This plan is the only authority for KAM2. Rows are KAM0-KAM5; they join the board when the
first crate lands. The arena goldens and `docs/FOCUS.md` "Now" stay untouched.

## What the game is

A real-time economy war game: every ware is a physical object carried by a worker along
roads the player lays; houses have work radii; production is a chain, not a timer; an AI
mayor runs the city and an AI general decides when the army is worth spending. Combat is
small-unit, formation-based, medieval.

Look: 2.5D. Orthographic isometric camera, pre-rendered 3D sprites on a 2:1 diamond grid,
8 facings, separate turret layer, team-colour mask tint, saturated baked sunlight, deep
shadows, chrome sidebar with cameos rendered from the same models. Red Alert 2 is the
reference, not the licence: all art is rendered here from CC0 sources.

## Legal line

- No KaM art, no KaM code, no AGPL text enters this repo.
- `_ref/kam_remake` is read for mechanics only. Every number is re-derived and written in
  `kam2-data`; the dossier records what was studied, not copied.
- Every model, texture and font is CC0, CC-BY (attributed) or rendered here.
- Kenney, Poly Haven and ambientCG are CC0 and may be committed as sources with their
  provenance rows. Quaternius is render-only: `docs/EVIDENCE.md` (content tracks B and D)
  records its QAL v1.0 terms as refused for git, so no Quaternius file is committed; only
  the sprite frames we render from it are, and those carry the render settings.

## Crates

New crates; nothing existing is rewritten. The arena stays the engine's proof game.

- `kam2-sim` - pure, std-only: tiles, roads, houses, wares, workers, orders, combat, tick.
  Same seed + same commands -> same hash. No IO, no GPU, no clock.
- `kam2-data` - rules tables: units, houses, wares, chains, costs, AI weights. Loaded from
  JSON generated by `tools/kam2/extract_rules.mjs`; hand-tuned numbers live here.
- `kam2-render` - the 2.5D pass over `engine-render`: ortho camera, diamond tile mesh with
  height steps, depth-sorted sprite quads, turret layer, team tint, fog/shroud.
- `kam2-ui` - sidebar, build menu with cameos, stock bar, minimap, selection, tooltips.
- `kam2-ai` - mayor (city) and general (army), reading only sim state.
- `kam2-game` - glue and the browser entry: setup, match loop, save, replay.
- `kam2-art` - not a crate: the Blender factory and its manifests under `content/kam2/`.

## Art pipeline (free, licence-clean)

Blender renders CC0 models; the repo already owns an atlas packer in spirit and the
engine owns a cooked bundle. Authoring rules live in `docs/plan/kam2-style-lock.md`.

1. Scene templates: infantry, vehicle with separate turret, building with three
   construction stages, terrain tile, cliff, water, tree, cameo.
2. One camera rig, one light rig, one render script, one export script.
3. Output: PNG sequences -> atlas + `manifest.json` (id, kind, pivot, footprint, turret
   anchor, facings, animations, mask atlas, cameo).
4. Sources: Quaternius (CC0), KayKit (CC0), Kenney (CC0), Poly Haven (CC0),
   game-icons.net (CC-BY 3.0), OFL fonts. FLUX.1-schnell (Apache-2.0) only for one-off
   images: portraits, banners, loading screens.

## Milestones and their proofs

M0 - the 2.5D proof. Ortho camera draws a 64x32 diamond field with one height step, one
sprite walking eight facings, one turret tracking the cursor. Proof: a windowed screenshot
plus `cargo test -p kam2-render --lib` green. No art volume yet.

M1 - the look lock. One soldier, one tank with turret, one house in three stages, grass,
dirt, water, cliff. Owner approves or redirects the style. Proof: the same scene as M0 with
real atlases; one screenshot, one frame-time number.

M2 - the economy. Roads, ten houses, wares, carriers, chains, warehouse, market, inn,
school, town hall. No combat. Proof: a 2000-tick headless sim hash, x2 identical; a played
build loop in the window.

M3 - the war. Eight units, formations, turret aiming, damage, death, garrison. Proof: a
scripted skirmish replay hash x2; a played skirmish.

M4 - the minds. AI mayor and general from the dossier; difficulty presets. Proof: 30
matches vs itself, no stalls, measured build order and army timing.

M5 - the browser. wasm build, one match vs AI at 30 fps 1080p on the RTX 3050. Proof: the
page plays a full match; frame receipt beside the native one.

## Order of work

M0 and the art factory start together: the factory can render while the renderer is built.
M2 follows M1 because the HUD needs the real sizes. M3 before M4. M5 last, after the
browser path's own gaps (WEB5, WEB6) are closed.

## Risks

- engine2040 has no orthographic projection and no sprite layer; M0 adds both.
- Its browser path still lists "plays the real match" as open; M5 inherits that work.
- Content volume is the long pole: 8 units x 8 facings x 4 animations, 10+ buildings x 3
  stages, terrain and UI. Waves, each reviewed.
- Art taste belongs to the owner: each wave is shown before volume continues.
