# EMPIRE — the real vision

Every agent in every baby reads this before its own plan. If a baby's plan is
smaller than this, the plan is wrong, not the vision.

Owner history cut to `lab/archive/docs/archive/empire-owner-history.md` — first read links there.

## The empire in 1–2 years (all four, at once)

1. **We ship our own games.** Players buy and play games built by our studios.
2. **We sell tools.** Forge is a paid product for Flax developers. engine2040 is open.
3. **The money machine pays for everything.** Steady income from many products.
4. **AI makes whole games.** A brief goes in; a finished, playable game comes out.

## The mix (2026-09-28; corrected 2026-09-28 per owner GO: reusable system first,
no imposed single audience, free engine, or Rome-only)

Each baby is a mix of many ideas; together they must be one great mix. The
test for any idea: does it make one of these three stronger for the same people?

- **Reusable system first:** before a big plan, a new repo or a vision, build
  the good reusable sprint loop and AI fleet (C-81..C-84).
  Simplify and improve forge without shrinking the engine vision.
- **No imposed audience:** itch.io first is where pages gather, not an owner
  constraint. Every release of any baby becomes a page, a GIF and a devlog there, so
  whoever follows one baby sees all three.
- **engine2040 is the proof and the pull.** MIT unchanged, pricing open: the Rome arena as a playable demo shows what an AI-built,
  2040-looking game feels like. It needs real art (CC0 sources and our own,
  never programmer art in a frame players see), a clean packaged build, and
  captures from the real GPU judged by eyes. Urban war stays the second game
  in its own plan; it is not cut by this section.
- **forge is the paid tool.** The AI studio for Flax developers, sold as early
  access as soon as its live golden paths pass (FINISH-LINE E2a), then grown.
  What its brief-to-game pipeline makes becomes free demos and paid templates.
- **The factory sells and tells.** Packs, tools and templates from the
  pipelines of both, in series; the pages, devlogs, trailers and bundles for
  everything (`os/lines/portfolio.json`).
- **Shared, not copied, not Rome-only:** Rome is the current shared art
  direction and asset set for the engine arena, forge's Flax arena and the
  factory's packs (our own art is sold; third-party CC0 is used, never resold).
  Urban war keeps its own scope in its plans. E3 uses the shared Roman art
  (`C:/forge/docs/finish-line/FINISH-LINE.md` E3); E2a early access starts when
  C1-C4 are met.
  The two MCP layers teach each other: the better one's tasks, evals and
  providers become the other's rows.
- **A great product, every baby:** someone outside downloads it and it works in
  15 minutes; eyes say it looks right next to its reference; and our own agents
  use it every day (engine and forge helpers work through their own MCP tools:
  dogfood), so its gaps show up in our own runs first.
- **The 10x bar (proof, not prose):** every product ships a stranger-15min proof
  (an outsider downloads and works in 15 minutes, timed), an eyes-vs-reference
  proof (captures judged by eyes next to the reference), a dogfood proof (our
  own agents use it daily through its own tools), price/bundle evidence from
  the sellers' own pages (links, never claims), a GIF-first devlog per release,
  a free Vol 0 per series and a bundle after 3 Vols. Marketing truth:
  UNKNOWN views = FAIL (UNKNOWN is never zero, never a pass), and PREP-ONLY
  is never shipped (a draft, a plan or a queue row is not a product).

## How the machine runs (current contract; execution measured separately)

- CURRENT installed mechanism (2026-09-28): when ON, each repo loops inside its own
  OpenCode desktop session with `/sprint`, 24/7, through its keeper with
   standing packet sets; current widths come from each repo's knobs.json
  (`center/knowledge/TEAMS.md`). Current helpers run on free models: Muse at
  xhigh for leads, judges, planners and the overseer (effort-appropriate
  reasoning where it fits); Ling only starts code whose output code checks.
  This describes the installed mechanism, not whether the owner's switch is ON.
- CURRENT foreground contract: the keeper plans packets;
  the lead sends one visible parallel foreground batch (`packet: <name>`),
  collects results and the independent judge chain, then dispatches again.
  Current commands and keeper code supersede the earlier C-88 staged wording.
  Installed code is not a claim of a successful unattended run: measure that
  from batch results, recovery tests and delivered outcomes. Widths are 20 in
  all four current knob files; knob values remain authoritative.
- Model mix target: free Muse effort-appropriate stays the
  workhorse; FIN / Bunny only after a paired eval proves fit for the role; a
  few paid Muse seats bounded with budget for critical blockers only. No paid
  config is installed by this file, and nothing here claims "only free forever".
- No new unsupported measures are claimed here (no quiet-hours, quotas, or
  utilization stats).
- A loop may stop only for: a needed review, a real blocker, no plans left, or
  everything perfect. Long context is not a stop: OpenCode compacts and the loop
  re-reads its board and handoff files, then continues.
- **Empire Boss** (`center`, `/empire` in OpenCode, Muse xhigh) sits above all
  three: knows this vision and what is really going on, moves wins between the
  babies through each repo's empire inbox (every loop reads its inbox at start
  and after every pass of its loop), flags drift and polish-only work, and
  writes `center/EMPIRE-STATUS.md` for the owner.
- Owner approves big pivots only. Everything else runs.

## Center done: the control plane at scale

Center is done when the owner can leave for a week and all three loops keep
landing judged PASS work: every ON loop running 24/7 through its keeper, the
watchdog GOing self-stopped loops within minutes, every drift FAIL routed to
its loop's inbox with numbers, every measured win spread to the other repos,
and measured upgrades when eligible (staged, judged, applied with checks before
and after, up to 6 per repo per day; a freeze needs his word). A verified
freeze or daily-limit wait is recorded without pretending an upgrade landed.
Useful delivery or evidence improvement, not a forced setup edit, moves each
round. Cost per judged PASS goes down
over time (`node empire.mjs metrics`); inbox backlogs stay within cap;
no loop waits on the owner except real blockers, big pivots and money out.
The reusable sprint loop and AI fleet scaffold is the first product of this
work: safe to generate offline, proof-carrying, never touching a live loop
until its integration is proven the same measured way.

## How the loops change themselves

The loops are meant to improve themselves and turn a bit when evidence says
so. What they may change, and what each step needs:

| Level | What changes | Needs | Who decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Board rows, queue tasks, their order | a research card or a finding | the loop, every pass |
| 2 | Agent rules and skills | the retro numbers (every 5 passes); kept only if the numbers move | the loop's judge (Muse xhigh) |
| 3 | Plans (plan files, finish-line steps, families) | a research idea that beat the current way in a real spike: numbers, not a paper claim | the judge + one other reviewer |
| 4 | The loop's own command, code laws, finish bars | evidence over at least 2 passes; bars only go up, never loosened to pass | the judge; auto-revert if the numbers drop in the next 5 passes |
| 5 | This vision, a baby's goal, killing a product family, money | a written case | Empire Boss proposes, owner approves |

- **Exploration budget:** about 1 in 10 Tasks every pass is a bet from
  research (a spike with a metric and a kill rule). A bet that wins moves up
  the ladder; that is how a loop changes direction.
- **Direction log:** every level 3 or 4 change is one line (date, level,
  change, evidence, revert trigger) in that repo's own log: engine2040's
  direction log, forge's Direction log table in its decisions doc, the
  factory's consensus board. The Empire Boss reads all three.
- **Never changed by a loop:** licensing law, safety and account rules, the
  determinism golden without a migration note, and "no fake done".
