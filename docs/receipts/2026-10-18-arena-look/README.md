# Arena look fixes, 2026-10-18

Three visual defects the demo agent found in the live arena window (`play-arena.bat`). Every
fix is presentation only: the sim hash and the arena digest are untouched (no sim crate edited).
Captures: `arena.exe --scene arena --fighters 2 --play --frames N --exit --capture-dir ...`
(window frame list through the offscreen twin), 960x540 downscales here. Before/after pairs are
the same sim tick. The live window (screenshot of the real swapchain) showed the same defects.

| # | defect | root cause | fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | dark discs on the sand in an X, none under the fighters (`before-wide-a.png`, `before-wide-b.png`) | `sun_contact_factor` in `crates/render/src/shaders/forward.wgsl` darkened sand within 1 m of 24 hard-coded world positions (old staged-duel fixtures: x = -8..10 at z = 0 and x = 2 at z = -9..9, each one a "whack-a-mole" disk added when a capture showed no contact). They never followed the fighters. | The feet are data now: `shadow.feet` (8 x vec4, behind the 32 sun bytes of binding 3) is written every frame from where the fighters stand (`Presenter::set_contact_feet`, fed by `FighterLook::present_into`). Soft smoothstep blob, fades out up the body. No fixed positions. `crates/render/src/contact_feet.rs` (pack + CPU oracle). |
| 2 | glowing white vertical streaks over the floor, white haze patches with straight edges (`before-wide-*.png` -> `after-look-a.png`, `after-look-b.png`) | Not particles (never drawn in the frame), not fog, not decals. The live IBL was baked from `polyhaven-studio-small-09-1k.hdr`, a photo studio: octagonal softboxes and a white cyclorama. The sand, stands and stone sample the prefiltered specular, so they reflected the softboxes (streaks, stretched by the normal-mapped sand) and the white cyclorama panels (hazy patches with straight edges). Proof: sun specular off changed nothing, IBL specular off removed them. | The live bake reads the arena's own day sky (`crates/render/src/ibl_arena_env.rs`, `bake_arena_day`, same `sky_radiance` the look and fog use), calibrated per channel to the old up-facing ambient so exposure and the tan of the sand hold. The 1.6 MB studio HDR is no longer embedded in the window build. |
| 3 | camera far away, fighters tiny at the screen edges (`before-wide-b.png`) | `fighter_look::fight_camera` took the side-on boom for two fighters and, when it lost a body (the spawn is 38 m apart: hero on the +x wall, rival in the far corner), `wide_group_camera` dollied out to hold both: two ~60 px figures. The Director's GP14 shoulder framing (`camera::frame_melee`, 40119424) was never called by the live window. | Exactly two seen fighters in the fight shot take the shoulder shot (`crates/game/src/fighter_look_shoulder.rs`, over `engine_combat::camera_framing::frame_melee`): eye behind the hero's shoulder, rival ahead. The wall shortens the boom (never the shoulder offset) so the eye stays in the pit, never on a body; the old side-on fit stays for 3+ fighters and when no shoulder holds both. Free camera (`C`) untouched. `after-spawn-shoulder.png` (hero pinned to the wall), `after-melee.png` (melee, both large, contact blobs under the feet). |

Tests: `contact_feet::tests::*`, `present::gpu_present::contact_live::tests::the_blob_follows_the_fed_feet_and_open_sand_has_no_fixed_discs` (GPU: no step edges on open sand, a fed foot darkens only its sand, clearing restores the open frame byte for byte), `ibl_arena_env::tests::*` (no spotty reflection at rough levels: peak/mean 3.0 to 3.4 against 54 to 62 for the studio; up-facing ambient per channel within 8 percent of the old), `fighter_look::fighter_look_shoulder::tests::*`, `fighter_look::tests::two_fighters_in_the_fight_shot_get_the_shoulder_camera`, `fighter_look::tests::contact_blobs_sit_under_the_seen_fighters_and_nowhere_else`. Not fixed in the three fixes above: movement keys were world axes (`mx` is +x, `mz` is +z in the sim), so the shoulder camera made W/A/S/D feel rotated and W at spawn ran into the pit wall. Fixed right after, see below.

## Camera-relative WASD, 2026-10-18

The window now rebuilds the human's wish every tick from where the live camera looks on the floor (`crates/game/src/camera_wish.rs`; `arena.rs` keeps the heading of the shot it just drew, `arena_frame::human_input` rotates the bindings' local (forward, right) wish onto the world axes the sim reads, a diagonal is scaled to one full step). W walks where the camera looks (at spawn: at the rival), S back, A and D strafe; the stick does the same. Input mapping only: no sim, `MatchSpec` or hashed byte changed (no sim crate edited; golden `d57b5c8ae3096614` and arena digest `d29b3b17bbd3b244` stand, and the staged-fight run prints the same `arena sim: ticks=600 hash=ef25059b432c9193` before and after), and the staged fight, the `--spear-demo` script and capture tapes keep their world axes. Free camera (`C`) does not steer.

`w-walk-a.png` and `w-walk-b.png` are one run, `arena.exe --scene arena --fighters 2 --play --spear --hold W --pause-at-tick 620 --frames 640 --exit --capture-dir target/captures/w-walk --capture-every 310` (`--hold W` holds the W key from the first tick on the keyboard's own road; `--pause-at-tick` runs one tick per frame). a is the spawn: hero pinned to the +x wall, rival a speck 38.6 m away. b is tick 620 (about 10 s of W and nothing else): the hero has run across the pit and the rival stands a few meters ahead, both large in the shoulder shot. Hero to rival gap by tick (`--view-brief`): 38.6 m at 0, 17 m at 200 (the straight line runs into the gate tunnel at x 7 to 13, the hero slides along its face and rounds it), 5 m at 600, in melee at 650. The old mapping sent the same W along world +x, into the pit wall 1.2 m from the hero's spawn spot.

Tests: `camera_wish::tests::*` (a camera looking down +z makes W go +z; all eight key directions at nine headings land where the renderer's own view matrix says; a diagonal is one full step), `fighter_look::fighter_look_shoulder::tests::w_at_the_live_spawn_heads_for_the_rival_and_not_into_the_wall` and `holding_w_in_the_shoulder_shot_walks_up_to_the_rival_from_any_side` (closed loop, the shot re-aims every step), arena bin `tests::w_walks_where_the_camera_looks_on_the_real_input_path`, `at_the_live_spawn_w_closes_on_the_rival_instead_of_running_into_the_wall`, `the_stick_walks_camera_relative_exactly_like_the_key`, `scripts_and_the_hand_off_tape_keep_their_world_axes_under_a_camera`, `the_title_tells_a_far_player_to_walk_and_goes_quiet_up_close`.

Found, not changed at that point: the window pinned the human input's `facing` to 0, so a standing `F` swung toward world +z and `Q` threw the spear toward world +z. Fixed right after, see below.

## Soft lock-on, 2026-10-18

The window now fills the human input's `facing` every tick with the bearing to the nearest living rival (`crates/game/src/lock_on.rs`: lower slot on a tie, rivals at 0 hp ignored; `arena.rs` `aim_at_rival` on the live keyboard and pad road only, off while the free camera `C` is flown). The live duel runs rules v2, so `F` swings and `Q` throws along the exact bearing. `spear_window::demo_input` uses the same `bearing_cdeg`. Input mapping only: no sim crate, `MatchSpec` or hashed byte changed (golden `d57b5c8ae3096614`, arena digest `d29b3b17bbd3b244`, staged fight `arena sim: ticks=600 hash=ef25059b432c9193` all stand).

Tests: `lock_on::tests::*` (eight relative positions, tie to the lower slot, dead rivals ignored, nobody to aim at), arena bin `tests::a_held_q_throws_at_the_rival_and_the_tether_forms_with_them` (the real input road: walk up with W, hold Q, the spear leaves along the bearing and sticks in the rival; the same bout with the free camera throws along world +z and misses) and `the_lock_on_sets_only_the_facing_and_the_free_camera_leaves_it_alone`.
